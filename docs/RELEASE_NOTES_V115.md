# v1.1.5 — bibliographic and submission closure

Version 1.1.5 is the closed submission artifact for **“Sharp Target-Domain
Certificates for Quantum-Kernel Advantage under Distribution Shift.”** It
contains the manuscript, Supplementary Information, sources, evidence chain,
tests, and integrity records prepared for manual submission to *npj Quantum
Information*.

Relative to immutable v1.1.4, this release adds no experiment, dataset,
analysis, theorem, or changed result. The final bibliographic pass positions
the fixed-batch certificate within partial identification; distinguishes it
from minimax transductive aggregation; and separates it from active,
multi-model, and out-of-distribution risk evaluation. It also records the
finite-shot complexity context and recent favorable QML evidence without
changing the study's claims. Bibliographic metadata was checked against
publisher, proceedings, and preprint records.

The central claim remains the sharp and minimal interval for the finite-batch
functional comparing a fixed candidate with the best member of a prespecified
fixed family under unrestricted completions of unaudited labels. The update is
pathwise exact after any realized audit subset, including adaptively selected
subsets.
